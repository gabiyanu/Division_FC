## 🎨 Design Inspiration

### Son of a Corner

**Author:** @sonofacorner  
**Repository:** https://github.com/sonofacorner/soc-viz-of-the-week  
**Twitter:** https://twitter.com/sonofacorner

The visualization styles in this project were heavily inspired by the excellent "Viz of the Week" series by Son of a Corner. The following visualization types were directly influenced by their work:

1. **Progressive Passes Grid** - Zone-based passing improvement visualization
2. **Crossing Zones Heatmap** - Half-pitch crossing origin maps
3. **Contested Zones** - Territorial control visualization
4. **Shot Maps** - Individual player shot location charts
5. **Touch Heatmaps** - Player activity density maps

We highly recommend checking out their work for more inspiration and learning.

## 🏷️ Brand Identity

### Division FC

The "Division FC" brand identity was created specifically for this project with the following design elements:

**Concept:** 
- Logo represents a division sign (÷) with eyes instead of dots
- Eyes symbolize "watching" and "analysis"
- Soccer crest shape for football context

**Color Palette - "Midnight Aurora":**
- Background: Deep Space Black (#0D1117)
- Primary Accent: Bright Cyan (#00FFCC)
- Secondary Accent: Hot Pink (#FF66B2)
- Tertiary Accent: Purple (#AA66FF)
- Highlight: Gold (#FFD700)

**Typography:**
- Primary: Inter / Arial Black (titles)
- Secondary: JetBrains Mono (statistics)
- Fallback: System sans-serif
